How we work

From brief to shortlist to placement.

Three steps give employers and candidates a shared view of what happens next.

  1. Step 01

    Agree on the brief

    Set the role scope, must have skills, work arrangement, and hiring timeline.

  2. Step 02

    Review the shortlist

    Compare candidates against the brief and agree on who to invite to interview.

  3. Step 03

    Coordinate the placement

    Share feedback, confirm offer details, and plan the transition into the role.

For candidates

Prepare for your next technical role.

A little preparation makes it easier to show how you work and decide whether a role fits what you want next.

Before your first conversation

  • Update your CV. Describe the projects you worked on and the decisions you owned.
  • Choose an example. Be ready to talk through a technical tradeoff and what you learned.
  • Share your preferences. Think about availability, role scope, and where you want to work.
  • Bring questions. Ask about the team, the work, and how the interview will run.
